# ES3
It's a Dev Board inspired by ESP32S3 DevkitC but has far more capability as it features a in-built 2.4inch TFT panel on its back , 3x Navigation Buttons , Micro SD Card slot & and in-built 1s Lipo Charging . It has the same configuration of USB as seen in ESP32S3 DevkitC i.e. One of them has direct USB and other has USB to UART conversion . It features a total of 19x GPIO's pins , 1x VCC (3.3v) ,3x VBUS (in) ,1x BAT + & 3x GND ports .
By the way the going merry is sailing on the back (ONE PIECE reference).
